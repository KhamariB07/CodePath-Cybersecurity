# Project 1: Catch Me if You Can!
### Overview
In this project, I handled my own Business Email Compromise situation, where a malicious actor (played by Leonardo DiCaprio) has sent phishing emails to many of our employees, trying to trick them into sending hundreds of thousands of dollars. To stop this, we'll be looking at some potentially malicious emails and inspecting .pcap files to determine which emails are legitimate and which ones are fraudulent.
